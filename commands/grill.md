Use the `agentic-decision-challenge` skill to stress-test the decision below.
**Load that skill and run its full loop** — do not grill from memory. That means:

asking only the settled-prerequisite frontier each round with a recommended answer
and a short menu of genuinely reasonable options; find discoverable facts yourself
instead of asking; route questions that need research or a prototype out to that
work; and stop at the shared-understanding gate without claiming authority to act.
